Questions

What CTOs ask before committing.

Straight answers — placeholder Q&A, to be replaced with Managed-Service-specific questions.

QDo we have to have Steamhaus build the estate first?
No. We can take on an estate we didn't build — the assessment maps its current state first, so we run it on facts, not assumptions. That said, the model works best when the same team's context carries through from build to run.
QIs this a helpdesk, or actual engineering?
Engineering. You get a named team of senior engineers who know your estate — not a rotating L1 queue reading from a runbook. AI tooling handles the monitoring volume; the judgment calls on incidents and change stay with our engineers.
QHow do you handle security and compliance?
We operate to ISO 27001 and are FSQS-registered, so regulated businesses — including financial services — can put our operations in front of an auditor. Patching, access control and audit trails are part of the standing service, not an add-on.
QAre we locked in?
No. The estate stays in your own AWS accounts, documented and yours. Managed Service is a relationship you choose to keep — if you want to bring operations in-house, everything's already in your hands and we help you transition.
