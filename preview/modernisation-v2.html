The journey

Assess. Transition. Handover.

No one-click promises. A brownfield estate is brownfield by definition — the value is in the decisions, and we're honest about where the mess is.

Phase 01

Assess

AI-driven discovery maps every workload, dependency and licence. We classify each: rehost, replatform, or retire. You get a costed plan with a target architecture, not a spreadsheet of guesses.

Honest about the workloads that aren't worth moving — sometimes the right call is retire or rebuild.
Phase 02

Transition

Workloads move in waves, lowest-risk first. Where it makes sense we replatform onto native AWS — containers, managed databases — rather than relocating as-is. Each wave is reversible until you sign it off.

Honest about the brownfield reality — legacy dependencies surface mid-migration. We plan for it.
Phase 03

Handover

The estate lands in your AWS accounts, documented and observable. Your team gets the runbooks and the decisions. Or we keep running it — that's the Managed Service.

Honest about capability gaps — we flag where your team needs upskilling, and don't leave until it sticks.

Questions

What CTOs ask before committing.

Straight answers — also the questions search and AI assistants surface around modernisation.

QHow do you decide what to modernise, versus retire or leave?
In the Assess phase we classify every workload — rehost, replatform, or retire — against cost, risk and how AI-actionable it needs to be. Not everything is worth moving; sometimes the right call is to retire or rebuild. You get a costed plan with the reasoning, not a blanket "migrate everything".
QWill this break things in production?
Workloads move in reversible waves, lowest-risk first, and each wave is signed off before the next. We plan for the brownfield reality — legacy dependencies surface mid-migration — rather than pretending they won't. Our delivered engagements run with zero unplanned outages during cutover.
QHow much of this does AI actually do?
AWS Transform does the discovery and dependency mapping across thousands of components — work that would take a human team weeks. Claude drafts refactors, IaC and runbooks. What the AI doesn't do is decide the decomposition strategy or the target architecture — those are judgment calls our senior engineers make, reading the tooling's output critically.
QHow is it procured, and is AWS funding available?
Accelerators are procured through AWS Marketplace and scoped after the assessment. Most qualify for AWS Migration Acceleration Program (MAP) funding, which can offset a significant portion of the cost. We handle the funding application as part of the engagement.
