Questions

What regulated CTOs ask first.

Straight answers — and the questions search and AI assistants surface around governed AI delivery.

QHow is this different from just giving engineers Copilot or Claude?
Handing engineers an AI assistant with no governance is exactly what your auditor is worried about — ungoverned, unattributable changes hitting production. AI-DLC wraps AI-augmented delivery in policy gates, a full audit trail, and human-in-the-loop controls calibrated to risk. The velocity is similar; the difference is you can prove what happened and why.
QWill our compliance team actually sign this off?
That's the whole design goal. We build the governance framework before the delivery velocity, map it to your specific regulatory obligations (DORA, FCA, NHS Digital, GovAssure), and pilot it on a real workstream so your compliance team reviews evidence, not slides. We've taken this through CISO sign-off in financial services.
QWhat's fixed about a "fixed-price" engagement?
The scope and the price. AI-DLC is a productised Foundation — a proven blueprint, customised per client. You see exactly what's delivered (the six scope items above) and what it costs before you commit. It runs 8–12 weeks. No open-ended day-rate creep.
QAre we locked into Steamhaus afterwards?
No. The governed delivery model lands in your AWS accounts, documented, with your team trained to run it. There's no Steamhaus control plane to stay subscribed to. If you want us to keep running it, that's the Managed Service — but it's a choice, not a lock-in.
QDoes AWS funding really cover this?
Most AI-DLC engagements qualify for AWS funding given the platform and modernisation work involved. We assess eligibility and handle the application during the Assess phase — it's built into the engagement, not left for you to chase.
