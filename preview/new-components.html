The problem

Most AWS estates weren't built for what's being asked of them now.

02

Entangled applications

Monoliths and legacy stacks that resist change — and that AI tooling can't usefully act on until they're modernised.

03

Cloud spend without control

Bills that climb faster than the value they return, with no clear line between what's essential and what's drift.
