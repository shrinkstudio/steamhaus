Questions

What CTOs ask before committing.

Straight answers — placeholder Q&A, to be replaced with Platform-specific questions.

QIs this a Steamhaus product we'd be locked into?
No. The platform is built in your own AWS accounts, on standard AWS — there's no proprietary control plane to stay subscribed to. When the engagement ends it's yours, documented and defensible to your CTO. If you want us to keep running it, that's the Managed Service — a choice, not a lock-in.
QDo we have to take all five elements?
No. The destination is one coherent platform, but each element can be scoped and delivered as its own fixed-price engagement. We'll tell you in the assessment which elements your estate needs first, and in what order.
QHow long does it take to stand up?
Weeks, not months, for a production-ready platform — the exact timeline depends on your estate and which elements you start with. You'll get a real timeline after the assessment, not a sales figure.
QHow is it procured, and is AWS funding available?
Procured through AWS Marketplace and scoped after the assessment. Most engagements qualify for AWS funding, which can offset a significant portion of the cost — we handle the application as part of the engagement.
