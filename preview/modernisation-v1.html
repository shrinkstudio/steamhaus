The premise

An architecture problem, not a code problem.

Anyone can throw an AI tool at a codebase. The value is in deciding what to decompose, what to keep, and what to retire — decisions that need judgment, across all three layers.

Modernisation that starts with code starts in the wrong place. The decomposition decisions — what becomes a service, what stays, what dies — are where the value is won or lost.

A monolith broken apart badly is just a distributed monolith — slower, harder to operate, no more AI-actionable than before. The architecture call comes first.

We work across all three layers: infrastructure (the estate itself), applications (the services that run on it), and data (the stores and pipelines underneath). Modernise one and ignore the others, and the gains leak away.
