Why three

Get AI-ready, stay AI-ready.

An AWS estate is a living system. The platform you build today gets modernised as the business changes, and operated as the platform underneath keeps moving. None of those three jobs is a one-off — and any one of them done in isolation breaks the next.

So we treat them as one offering, structured as three. Build it. Modernise it. Run it. Each pillar productised where it can be, judgment-led where it can't.
